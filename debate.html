Udbhav 2027

20th National Inter-University Debate Competition · January 14-15, 2027
Debate Motion / वाद-विवाद का विषय
"AI-led development is compatible for generating mass employment opportunities for Indian youth."
"कृत्रिम बुद्धिमत्ता आधारित विकास भारतीय युवाओं के लिये व्यापक रोजगार के अवसर सृजित करने में संगत है।"

Awards & Recognition

Udbhav provides a nationwide competitive stage to celebrate debate eloquence. Outstanding speakers are honored with prestigious awards, trophies, and cash prizes to recognize their debating talent.

Winner Prize
Rs. 5,000
Runner Up
Rs. 3,000

Additionally, each category (Hindi & English) will feature:

  • A prestigious Winner's Shield awarded to the top university team
  • Two Consolation Prizes for outstanding speakers
  • Four Special Recognition Prizes in each language category
  • Official Participation Certificates awarded to all contest delegates

Note: The Pantnagar University host team competes for individual speaker prizes only.

Debate Rules & Guidelines

  • Bilingual Contest Structure: The debate is conducted in Hindi and English separately on the campus of G.B. Pant University of Agriculture & Technology.
  • Team Representation: Each participating university is entitled to enter two teams of two delegates each (one Hindi, one English team). In each team, one member speaks for and one member speaks against the topic.
  • Fostering New Talents: To constantly promote and encourage new speakers, any previous participants who have secured first position in this contest in past years are not eligible to compete.
  • Time Allotment: Each speaker is allotted 5 + 1 minutes. Immediately following each speech, there will be a Q&A session.
  • Question & Answer Round: The questions from judges/house will be restricted to 1 minute, and the speaker's response must be completed in the next 2 minutes.
  • No Written Speeches: Speakers reading from pre-written text will not be eligible for prizes. A single slip of paper bearing only brief key bullet points is permitted.
  • Final Adjudication: The decisions of the judging panel shall be final and binding on all participating universities.
  • Registration Deadline: The final date for debate team registration is December 05, 2026.
