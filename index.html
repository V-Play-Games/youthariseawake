Welcome to YUVA 2026

Man-Making & Nation-Building

Greetings from Vivekanand Swadhyay Mandal, Pantnagar, a registered professional body of Pantnagar Students, working incessantly for youth empowerment for the last 26 years. It is a group of committed and awakened students and successful professionals of G.B. Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar who strive for excellence among individuals and society, having espoused Swami Vivekananda as their role model.

The National Youth Awakening Festival, YUVA is the most spectacular activity of Vivekanand Swadhyay Mandal, being organized for the last 20 years continuously on National Youth Day (Swami Vivekanand Jayanti), on a mass scale, where young minds from different parts of India come together for the involvement, development and discussion about the situations that youth face for the advancement of the nation.

Flagship Events

Discover our two major national scale events for YUVA 2026

National Youth Conference

Manthan

January 12-13, 2026

Manthan is a premium forum designed to encourage constructive social dialogues around subjects of prime importance. Conference participants have the option to present research papers, make poster presentations, or participate as active delegates in keynotes by eminent leaders.

Conference Theme

"Cultural Roots and Digital Power: Guiding Youth for Cultural Strengthening and Nation Building with Technological Innovations."

Learn More Register
Inter-University Debate

Udbhav

January 14-15, 2026

Gathering representing speakers from over 50 universities across India. Udbhav represents a major platform to debate, deliberate and dialogue on complex sociocultural topics, testing public speaking, logic, and reasoning capabilities of the youth.

Debate Topic

"Digital economy is fully capable of fulfilling the real needs of Indian youth."
